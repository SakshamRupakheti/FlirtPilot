Source: https://huggingface.co/datasets/allenai/soda

License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . Supplied as-is without warranties; see the license's disclaimer of warranties and limitation of liability. No endorsement is implied.
